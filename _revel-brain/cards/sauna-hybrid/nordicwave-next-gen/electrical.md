# NordicWave (Next Gen) · Electrical

**Product:** Revel NordicWave Infrared & Traditional Hybrid Sauna - Next Gen  
**SKUs:** `R-CS-4P-V2`, `R-CS-4P-V2-H-SW45KW`, `R-CS-4P-V2-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

### ELECTRICAL (excluding Harvia heater)

- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 3230 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp
- **SAA:** SAA® certified for Australian standards.

### ELECTRICAL (For Harvia heater sold Separately)

- Consult the listing of the heater you have selected for power specifications, the Harvia heaters must be installed by a certified electrician and do not come with Power cords.

### Heater options by SKU

_From the heater cards. The electrician confirms the circuit on site._

| SKU | Heater | Power | Circuit | Controller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R-CS-4P-V2-H-SW45KW` | Harvia Manual Black Wall Heater 4.5kW - SW45 | 4500 Watts | 20 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Built-in (manual heater) |
| `R-CS-4P-V2-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WIFI` | Harvia Electronic Black Wall Heater 4.5kW - SW45E | 4500 Watts | 20 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Harvia Xenio Heater Control Kits - Wifi |

## Customer-ready answer

The NordicWave (Next Gen) runs on 220–240 V, draws 3,230 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
