# Outdoor NordicWave 3P · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Outdoor NordicWave 3 Person Infrared & Traditional Hybrid Sauna  
**SKUs:** `R-CS-CAB-3P`, `R-CS-CAB-3P-H-SW45KW`, `R-CS-CAB-3P-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WIFI`, `R-CS-CAB-3P-H-SW60KW`, `R-CS-CAB-3P-H-SW60KW-E-WIFI-XENIO`, `R-CS-CAB-3P-H-SW90KW-E-XENIO-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 3230 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp
- **SAA:** SAA® certified for Australian standards.

### ELECTRICAL (For Harvia heater & Xenio both sold Separately)

- **Heater Option 1:** Wall Heater 4.5kw
- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Power:** 4500 Watts
- **Socket:** 20 Amp (dedicated circuit)
- **Installation:** By a licensed electrician only
- **Heater Option 2:** Wall Heater 6.0kw
- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Power:** 6000 Watts
- **Socket:** 32 Amp (dedicated circuit)
- **Installation:** By a licensed electrician only

## Customer-ready answer

The Outdoor NordicWave 3P runs on 220–240 V, draws 3,230 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
