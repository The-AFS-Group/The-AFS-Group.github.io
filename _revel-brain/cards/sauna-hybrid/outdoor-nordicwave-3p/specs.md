**Product:** Revel Outdoor NordicWave 3 Person Infrared & Traditional Hybrid Sauna

## Key facts

- **Material:** 40mm Japanese Cedar (Sugi)
- **Glass:** 8mm Tinted Tempered Glass
- **Roof:** Asphalt Bitumen Shingles
- **Handle:** Japanese Cedar Internal / Stainless Steel External
- **Ventilation:** Top and bottom air circulation vents
- **Control Panel:** LED WIFI Touch Panel
- **Infrared Heaters:** 8 Dual-Mode Full Spectrum & Far Infrared Heaters
- **Lighting:** LED Multi Colour
- **Audio:** Built-in Revel Rhythm Vibration Bluetooth Speakers
- **Infrared Temperature Range:** 30 to 70°C
- **Traditional Temperature Range:** 60 to 85°C
- **Traditional Temperature Range with Xenio Controller:** 50 to 110°C

- Heater Drip Tray
