## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 2980 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp
- **SAA:** SAA® certified for Australian standards.

## Customer-ready answer

The Aura 2P (Next Gen) runs on 220–240 V, draws 2,980 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
