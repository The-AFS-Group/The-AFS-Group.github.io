## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 2260 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp

## Customer-ready answer

The Kora 3P (Next Gen) runs on 220–240 V, draws 2,260 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
