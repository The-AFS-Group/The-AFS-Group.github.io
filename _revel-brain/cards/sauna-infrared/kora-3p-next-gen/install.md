## Facts

### Installation & clearance

- The Kora is designed for Residential use only

### Minimum Clearances Required

- 100mm Rear and Sides
- 300mm Overhead

## Customer-ready answer

The Kora 3P (Next Gen) is designed for residential use only. It needs at least 100 mm clearance at the rear and sides, 300 mm overhead and unobstructed access at the front. Don't install it in an enclosed space or a commercial setting: installing outside these requirements voids the warranty.
