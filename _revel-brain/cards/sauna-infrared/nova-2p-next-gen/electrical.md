# Nova 2P (Next Gen) · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Nova 2 Person Full Spectrum Infrared Sauna - Next Gen  
**SKUs:** `R-FS-PREM-2P-V2`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 2500 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Lighting:** LED Lamp and LED Strip Light
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp
- **SAA:** SAA® certified for Australian standards.

## Customer-ready answer

The Nova 2P (Next Gen) runs on 220–240 V, draws 2,500 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
