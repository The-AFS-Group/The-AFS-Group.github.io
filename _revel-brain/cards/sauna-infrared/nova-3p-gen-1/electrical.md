# Nova 3P (Gen 1) · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Nova (Gen 1) - 3 Person Full Spectrum Infrared Cedar Sauna  
**SKUs:** `R-FS-PREM-3P`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Temp Range:** 0-70°C
- **Voltage:** 220 - 240 Volts
- **Power:** 3000 Watts
- **Socket:** 15 Amp Plug
- **Lighting:** LED Lamp and LED Strip Light
- **Electrician Required?:** Yes - to upgrade socket if only currently 10 Amp

## Customer-ready answer

The Nova 3P (Gen 1) runs on 220–240 V, draws 3,000 W and plugs into a 15 amp power point. If the power point is a standard 10 amp outlet, a licensed electrician needs to upgrade it to 15 amp first.
