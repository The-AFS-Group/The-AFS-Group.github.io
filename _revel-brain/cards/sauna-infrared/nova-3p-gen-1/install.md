# Nova 3P (Gen 1) · Install

**Product:** Revel Nova (Gen 1) - 3 Person Full Spectrum Infrared Cedar Sauna  
**SKUs:** `R-FS-PREM-3P`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

### Installation & clearance

- The Nova sauna is approved for use in both residential and commercial environments, provided all installation, clearance, operational, and maintenance requirements outlined below are strictly adhered to.

### Minimum Clearances Required

- 100mm Rear and Sides
- 300mm Overhead
- Unobstructed Front Access
- The sauna must be installed in a well-ventilated, non-enclosed space in accordance with these requirements. Failure to comply with the specified installation, clearance, operational, and maintenance requirements , including installation in enclosed spaces or environments that do not meet ventilation standards may result in unsafe operation and will void the manufacturer’s warranty.

### Commercial operators are responsible for ensuring

- Routine maintenance is performed at prescribed intervals by a registered sauna technician
- All electrical work, inspections, and servicing are carried out by a licensed electrician in accordance with applicable regulations
- A sauna usage log is maintained to accurately track operational hours, servicing, and maintenance activities
- More detailed information regarding sauna installation, operation, and maintenance in a commercial environment can be provided by your Revel Sales Representative.

## Customer-ready answer

The Nova 3P (Gen 1) is designed for residential use only. It needs at least 100 mm clearance at the rear and sides, 300 mm overhead and unobstructed access at the front. Don't install it in an enclosed space or a commercial setting: installing outside these requirements voids the warranty.
