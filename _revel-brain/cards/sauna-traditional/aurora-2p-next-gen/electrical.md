# Aurora 2P (Next Gen) · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Aurora - 2 Person Traditional Cedar Barrel Sauna - Next Gen  
**SKUs:** `R-BT-CEDAR-2P-V2-H-BC35KW`, `R-BT-CEDAR-2P-V2-H-SW45KW`, `R-BT-CEDAR-2P-V2-H-BC35KW-E-XENIO-VEGA-WIFI`, `R-BT-CEDAR-2P-V2-H-BC35KW-E-XENIO-VEGA-NON-WIFI`, `R-BT-CEDAR-2P-V2-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Lighting:** LED Strip Light
- **LED Power Supply:** 10A
- **Note:** This sauna doesn't come with a heater, power cord or plug for the heater. The heater is sold as an add-on and must be wired and installed by a qualified electrician. The LED lighting operates off a separate standard 10 Amp power plug, which comes connected ready to use.

## Customer-ready answer

The cabin's LED lighting runs off a standard 10 amp power point. The Harvia heater is sold separately, has no plug, and must be hard-wired by a licensed electrician on its own circuit. The circuit depends on the heater: a 3.5 kW heater needs a 15 amp dedicated circuit and a 4.5 kW heater a 20 amp circuit.
