# Eden 8P · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Eden - 8 Person Traditional Barrel Sauna  
**SKUs:** `R-BT-8P-V2-H-SW60KW`, `R-BT-8P-V2-H-SW60KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Lighting:** LED Strip Light
- **LED Power Supply:** 10A
- **Note:** This sauna doesn't come with a heater, power cord or plug for the heater. The heater is sold as an add-on and must be wired and installed by a qualified electrician. The LED lighting operates off a separate standard 10 Amp power plug, which comes connected ready to use.

### Heater options by SKU

_From the heater cards. The electrician confirms the circuit on site._

| SKU | Heater | Power | Circuit | Controller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R-BT-8P-V2-H-SW60KW` | Harvia Manual Black Wall Heater 6kW - SW60 | 6000 Watts | 32 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Built-in (manual heater) |
| `R-BT-8P-V2-H-SW60KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI` | Harvia Electronic Black Wall Heater 6kW - SW60E | 6000 Watts | 32 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Harvia Xenio Heater Control Kits - Wifi |

## Customer-ready answer

The cabin's LED lighting runs off a standard 10 amp power point. The Harvia heater is sold separately, has no plug, and must be hard-wired by a licensed electrician on its own circuit. The circuit depends on the heater: a 6 kW heater needs a 32 amp dedicated circuit.
