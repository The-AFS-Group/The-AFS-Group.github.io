# Kuusi 4P · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Kuusi 4 Person Traditional Finnish Sauna  
**SKUs:** `R-TR-VAL-4P-H-SW45KW`, `R-TR-VAL-4P-H-SW60KW`, `R-TR-VAL-4P-H-SW45KW-E-WIFI-XENIO`, `R-TR-VAL-4P-H-SW60KW-E-WIFI-XENIO`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Lighting:** LED Strip Light
- **LED Power Supply:** 10A
- **Note:** This sauna doesn't come with a heater, power cord or plug for the heater. The heater is sold as an add-on and must be wired and installed by a qualified electrician. The LED lighting & Ventilation operates off a separate standard 10 Amp power plug, which comes connected ready to use.

### ELECTRICAL (For Harvia heater sold Separately)

- Consult the listing of the heater you have selected for power specifications, the Harvia heaters must be installed by a certified electrician and do not come with Power cords.

## Customer-ready answer

The cabin's LED lighting and ventilation runs off a standard 10 amp power point. The Harvia heater is sold separately, has no plug, and must be hard-wired by a licensed electrician on its own circuit. The circuit depends on the heater: a 4.5 kW heater needs a 20 amp dedicated circuit and a 6 kW heater a 32 amp circuit.
