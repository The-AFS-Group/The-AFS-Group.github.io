# Loom 2P (Next Gen) · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Loom 2 Person Traditional Finnish Sauna - Next Gen  
**SKUs:** `R-TR-PREM-2P-H-SW45KW`, `R-TR-PREM-2P-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

### ELECTRICAL (excluding Harvia heater)

- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Lighting:** LED Strip Light
- **LED Power Supply:** 10A
- **Note:** This sauna doesn't come with a heater, power cord or plug for the heater. The heater is sold as an add-on and must be wired and installed by a qualified electrician. The LED lighting operates off a separate standard 10 Amp power plug, which comes connected ready to use.

### ELECTRICAL (For Harvia heater sold Separately)

- Consult the listing of the heater you have selected for power specifications, the Harvia heaters must be installed by a certified electrician and do not come with Power cords.

### Heater options by SKU

_From the heater cards. The electrician confirms the circuit on site._

| SKU | Heater | Power | Circuit | Controller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R-TR-PREM-2P-H-SW45KW` | Harvia Manual Black Wall Heater 4.5kW - SW45 | 4500 Watts | 20 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Built-in (manual heater) |
| `R-TR-PREM-2P-H-SW45KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI` | Harvia Electronic Black Wall Heater 4.5kW - SW45E | 4500 Watts | 20 Amp (dedicated circuit) | Harvia Xenio Heater Control Kits - Wifi |

## Customer-ready answer

The cabin's LED lighting runs off a standard 10 amp power point. The Harvia heater is sold separately, has no plug, and must be hard-wired by a licensed electrician on its own circuit. The circuit depends on the heater: a 4.5 kW heater needs a 20 amp dedicated circuit.
