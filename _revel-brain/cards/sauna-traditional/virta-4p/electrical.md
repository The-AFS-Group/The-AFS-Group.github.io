# Virta 4P · Electrical

**Product:** Revel Virta 4 Person Traditional Cabin  
**SKUs:** `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-H-SW60KW`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-S-019`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-H-SW60KW-E-XENIO-WALL-WIFI`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-H-PC70-E-BLK-XENIO-WIFI`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-H-PC70-E-XENIO-WIFI-CX170`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-S-019-XENIO-WIFI-CX170`, `R-TR-VIRTA-4P-H-SW90KW-E-XENIO-WIFI`  
**Markets:** AU

## Facts

- **Voltage:** 220 – 240 Volts
- **Lighting:** LED Strip Light
- **LED Lights:** 28 Watts TBC
- **LED Power Supply:** 10A
- **Note:** This sauna doesn't come with a heater, power cord or plug for the heater. The heater is sold as an add-on and must be wired and installed by a qualified electrician. The LED lighting operates off a separate standard 10 Amp power plug, which comes ready to use.

## Customer-ready answer

The cabin's LED lighting runs off a standard 10 amp power point. The Harvia heater is sold separately, has no plug, and must be hard-wired by a licensed electrician on its own circuit. The circuit depends on the heater: a 6 kW heater needs a 32 amp dedicated circuit, a 7 kW heater a 32 amp circuit and a 9 kW heater a 37.5 amp circuit.
