# EMF test report T1881542 02 · Infrared sauna

**Applies to:** to be confirmed (file name says "SK")  
**Markets:** AU, NZ

## Facts

- **Report number:** T1881542 02
- **Test lab:** Shenzhen Alpha Product Testing Co., Ltd.
- **Equipment tested:** Infrared sauna, 1600 W, AC 230 V 50 Hz (one model tested for a range of 30 models that differ only in name)
- **Standard:** EN 62233:2008, measurement methods for electromagnetic fields of household appliances and similar apparatus with regard to human exposure
- **Sample received:** 27 September 2018
- **Test date:** 25 October 2018
- **Report date:** 24 July 2019
- **What was measured:** Magnetic field emission, 10 Hz to 400 kHz, time domain evaluation (clause 5.5.2)
- **Measuring distance:** 10 cm
- **Operating condition:** Continuously running
- **Limit:** 100% (reference level)
- **Result:** Pass

### Results (% of the limit)

| Sensor location | Measured | With measurement uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Front | 1.171% | 1.185% |
| Rear | 1.162% | 1.176% |
| Left | 1.163% | 1.177% |
| Right | 1.189% | 1.204% |

## Customer-ready answer

<!-- Ben to approve the wording. Only for the products in applies_to. -->

An independent lab tested it to EN 62233, the European method for measuring the electromagnetic fields household appliances give off. At 10 cm from the outside of the cabin, the magnetic field measured about 1.2% of the reference limit: a pass.

## Staff notes

- Which Revel products this report covers must be confirmed; `applies_to` is empty until then.
- The tested unit drew 1600 W. Revel's current infrared saunas draw 1,960 to 3,020 W (Kora 2P to Luna 3P). Confirm the current models are covered by this 2018 test before quoting it for them.
- Readings were taken outside the cabin only (front, rear, left, right); this report has no inside readings.
- The report says it "shall not be reproduced in parts without written approval" of the lab. Quote the result; don't send extracts.
- EN 62233 measures low-frequency magnetic fields only. It doesn't support "zero EMF" or claims about electric fields or health effects.
- Small errors in the report itself: the sensor location reads "Tools, transportable" (template text), and its model list repeats one model and lists another twice.
