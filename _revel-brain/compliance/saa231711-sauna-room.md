## Staff notes

- The certificate names the manufacturer's model numbers, not Revel product names. Which Revel saunas it covers must be confirmed before this card goes live; `applies_to` is empty until then.
- The rating is 3-6 kW. Several Revel saunas are sold with a 9 kW heater (SW90 and `s-019` variants of the Loom 4P, Tampere 4P, Solaris, Virta 4P and Tampere Grand). If this certificate covers any of those cabins, confirm the 9 kW configuration is covered before quoting it.
- Re-check before 15 November 2028 (expiry).
