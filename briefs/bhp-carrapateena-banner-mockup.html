Notes for Joe

Hero is the prize. Source is the G15 Starter Package render (Day08-HeroImage), which shows all four items in one shot: rack, plates, barbell, bench. It is the whole prize in a single image.

The PACKAGE flash is stripped. The source render (Day08-HeroImage) carries a full-width orange "PACKAGE" merchandising band across the bottom, and the bench feet sit inside it, so it cannot just be cropped off. I removed it by neutralising the orange, since the product itself is entirely black and chrome. For print, get the clean render from the Force USA source files rather than reusing this.

Product sits on white, so the hero and the copy block below are one continuous white field. The black logo bar at the top and the orange and black bands at the bottom are what frame it.

Scan the QR. It is the real production file and resolves to the live entry page.

QR height. Lands roughly 580 to 910mm off the floor. My brief originally said 1,000 to 1,400mm. That was wrong and it does not survive this layout, or the Hyrox one it is based on, which sits in the same place. Treat 600 to 1,000mm as the real target.

BHP logo is a placeholder set in Montserrat. Still needs the real mark from Tyson.
