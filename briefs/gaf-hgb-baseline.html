The metric · locked 4 Aug 2026

Three qualifying paths

A cart is a Home Gym Builder build if it clears the $3,376 value gate and satisfies any one of the three paths. The anchor requirement keeps it a genuine home-gym measure; Path 1 is deliberately limited to the two centrepiece categories that define a home gym.

Path 1 · Centrepiece

An All-In-One or Home Gym

A single anchor over the gate, but only if it is an All-In-One Trainer or a Home Gym / Multi-Station unit. A lone treadmill or bike does not qualify on its own.

AIO or Home Gym  +  cart > $3,376
180 qualify on this path alone (FY26)
Path 2 · Anchor + attachment

Any anchor, kitted out

Any anchor product plus at least one attachment item, over the gate. The everyday build.

1 anchor  +  1 attachment  +  cart > $3,376
832 of the 1,257 builds (FY26)
Path 3 · Multi-anchor

Two centrepieces in one cart

Two or more anchors over the gate, regardless of attachment count.

2+ anchors  +  cart > $3,376
245 of the 1,257 builds (FY26)

Change on 4 Aug: Path 1 was tightened from "any anchor" to "All-In-One or Home Gym only". A single big-ticket treadmill, bike, rower or rack no longer counts on its own; it needs an attachment (Path 2) or a second anchor (Path 3). This is the more defensible line for a home-gym metric.

What is inside the number

1,257 builds, and the carts the rule sets aside

Of the 1,708 FY26 GAF AU carts that clear the $3,376 value gate, the three-path rule passes 1,257. The 451 it turns away are carts with no anchor at all, or a single non-centrepiece anchor with nothing built around it.

Method & assumptions

How the number was built

So the team can trust, challenge and reproduce it.

Rule. Locked with Adam Carter 4 Aug 2026: a cart qualifies over $3,376 if it holds an All-In-One or Home Gym anchor (Path 1), any anchor plus an attachment (Path 2), or two or more anchors (Path 3). Read-only throughout.
Window. FY26 baseline is 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Jun 2026. The dashboard tracker counts cumulatively from 1 Jul 2025 toward the 10,000 BHAG.
Scope. Sales orders on the GAF AU + GAF Online-AU departments (matching NetSuite saved search 6022 / GAF BHAG Data), excluding cancelled and closed orders, and GAF Commercial, Wholesale, Warranty, NZ and Export.
Composition. Line-level, priced product lines only (kit parents once, tax and shipping dropped), each SKU mapped to its sub-category, tagged anchor / attachment / neither, and the three-path rule applied. Attachments counted by distinct SKU.
Daily tracking. A BHAG tracker on the GAF master dashboard reads the current cumulative count and the 10,000 target; the underlying number is refreshed from this same read-only pull.
