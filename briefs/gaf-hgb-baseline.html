What counts as a
Home Gym Builder sale

The definition locked with Adam Carter on 4 Aug 2026: a qualifying sale is any cart over $3,376 that contains a training anchor. Run over the last 12 months of GAF AU retail trade, the baseline is 1,408 Home Gym Builder sales a year, 98% of them consumers. This is the number we grow toward the 30,000-by-2030 BHAG.

The metric · locked 4 Aug 2026

Three qualifying paths

A cart is a Home Gym Builder sale if it clears the $3,376 value gate and satisfies any one of the three paths below. In practice the three collapse to a single test, since Path 1 already contains the other two: a value-cart qualifies whenever it holds at least one training anchor.

Path 1 · Single anchor

One centrepiece over the gate

One anchor product with a total cart value over the threshold, no attachment required.

1 anchor  +  cart > $3,376
495 qualify on this path alone (single anchor, under two attachments)
Path 2 · Anchor + attachment

One centrepiece, kitted out

One anchor plus one or more attachment items, over the threshold. The everyday build.

1 anchor  +  1 attachment  +  cart > $3,376
660 carts pair an anchor with two or more attachments
Path 3 · Multi-anchor

Two centrepieces in one cart

Two or more anchors over the threshold, regardless of attachment count.

2+ anchors  +  cart > $3,376
253 carts carry two or more anchors

Change from the prior baseline: the earlier rule required an anchor plus two attachments (or two anchors). The three-path rule, locked with Adam on 4 Aug, admits a single anchor over $3,376 on its own. That is the lever that moves the number.

Method & assumptions

How the number was built

So the team can trust, challenge and reproduce it.

Rule. Locked with Adam Carter 4 Aug 2026: a cart qualifies if its total is over $3,376 and it contains at least one training anchor (Path 1 single anchor, Path 2 anchor plus attachment, Path 3 two or more anchors). Read-only throughout.
Scope. Sales orders on the GAF AU + GAF Online-AU departments (matching the standing NetSuite saved search 6022), dated the last 12 months, excluding cancelled and closed orders. This excludes GAF Commercial, Wholesale, Warranty, NZ and Export. ~15,400 orders.
Value gate. Order total over $3,376, inclusive of GST and freight (the cart value the customer is charged). 1,725 orders clear it.
Composition. For each cart, the priced order lines were taken (kit parents counted once, exploded component and box lines collapsed to the parent, tax and shipping lines dropped), each distinct product mapped to its sub-category, then tagged anchor / attachment / neither and the three-path rule applied. Attachments counted by distinct SKU.
Coverage. The bulk of priced lines matched the categorisation directly; a tail of bundle and variant SKUs (REP racks and Ares trainers, STEPR cardio, Impulse machines, flooring and plate bundles) were classified from their NetSuite descriptions. Unresolved lines were treated as "neither", the conservative direction.
Daily tracking. A BHAG tracker on the GAF master dashboard reads the current rolling count and target. The underlying count is refreshed from this same read-only pull; see the dashboard for the live figure.
