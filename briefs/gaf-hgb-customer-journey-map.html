01 · How to read this map

Two things make this different from a funnel

This is the actual journey of GAF's most profitable customer — reconstructed from real calls, live performance data and the unit economics, then read through JTBD and the McKinsey loyalty loop.

Self-Serve lane

It runs in two lanes

The Home Gym Builder buys one of two ways, and the strategy depends on both. The Self-Serve lane is online, led by the AI Gym Designer. The same person can cross lanes mid-journey — and the highest-value ones usually do.

Sales-Assisted lane

It ends where it begins

The Sales-Assisted lane is led by the phone consultant. Advocacy is not the last stage — it is the top of the next customer's Consideration. The referral loop is the engine of the 30,000-gym BHAG.

02 · Who this is (and is not)

Why this customer is the whole strategy

The Home Gym Builder is a project-mode buyer kitting out a garage or spare room — a household decision, $4k+ project value. Persona anchor: David Fowler (built from survey data, not a real customer). The locked "Home Gym Builder sale" (Adam Carter): 1 anchor product + 2 attachment SKUs + total cart over $3,376.

Load-bearing refusal

This map is NOT budget-conscious single-SKU shoppers. They get self-serve only — no consultant time, no freight subsidy. The Home Gym Builder is the only segment that makes money online; every other online archetype loses money without a consultant. The journey must never quietly widen to "for everyone."

05 · Stage by stage

The detail, with the customer's own words

Every quote below is verbatim from the coded Home Gym Builder sales calls.

00Triggerthe thing that starts it

What the data shows. The dominant triggers are a life or project change (renovation, a new house), quitting or being priced out of a commercial gym, and a promotional event — Black Friday appears repeatedly as the timing trigger. Discovery is heavily existing-customer and referral: the loyalty loop already turning.

"we're just in the middle of our renos"
"I need to do something… but I'm never gonna go to a gym"
GAF action

Be present at the trigger: retargeting for renovators/movers, always-on brand for the "quit the gym" moment, and a promotional calendar that gives fence-sitters a date. Protect the referral loop — it feeds this stage for free.

Metric: Triggered sessions and inbound call volume by source.
01Consideration"I just don't know what I want"

State of mind: overwhelmed. The single most revealing pattern is decision paralysis colliding with hard spatial constraint.

"I just don't know what I want."

"most of the garage here in Australia is sloped down."

Dominant JTBD: Convenience / Efficiency (503 of 724 Consideration calls), with Achievement / Progress close behind. Household is in the room: 15% of decisions are joint-equal, and a spouse or partner is named throughout — a two-person decision, not one.

GAF action — the wedge

This is the exact stage the AI Gym Designer is built to rescue. "I don't know what I want" + "will it fit my sloped 24-inch garage" is a design problem. The Designer turns an overwhelmed browser into a person with a plan.

Metric: Session-to-designer-start rate; call-to-qualified-lead rate.
02Design"show me my gym before I spend a cent"

State of mind: ready to be led. When someone else takes the design burden, the mood flips from anxious to excited. The tell from the Sales-Assisted lane:

"I had a plan to finish off my gym, now you've given me some different things to think about."

That is the consultant expanding a single-item enquiry into a full project — the single highest-leverage move in the whole journey.

Touchpoints. Self-Serve: the AI Gym Designer (upload the space → real-time plan → anchor + 2+ attachments → saved, shareable design). Sales-Assisted: the consultant builds the same picture live on the call.

GAF action — the front door

Brand promise 01 ("You'll talk to a designer", now AI-powered) lives here. The Designer's job is to produce a cart matching the locked HGB definition, so the "design" and the "sale" are the same object. Save every design — it powers Grow and Advocacy later.

Metric: Number of designs started and saved (the 100-designs-by-1-Oct milestone measures exactly this).
03Purchase"just the shipping, and is it in stock?"

State of mind: positive, close to committed. Decision-stage sentiment is overwhelmingly positive (160 of 173 calls). The big objections have collapsed to three small ones: shipping cost, stock availability, and final price.

"Just the shipping, probably."
"I just wanted to check that on stock before I made the drive down for you, boys."

The two lanes at the moment of truth. Self-Serve: freight and stock must be visible and honest before checkout (shipping-cost surprise and stock ambiguity are the top website-confusion topics). Sales-Assisted: the consultant closes with a quote, freight solution, finance option and local pickup. Our advantages: stock availability, local pickup, a discount / free-shipping offer. Our disadvantages: high shipping cost and higher price vs competitor. Purchase is won by neutralising freight.

Metric: Design-to-purchase conversion; AOV (target above the $3,376 threshold).
04Build"it arrived, now help me set it up right"

State of mind: excited but daunted by a pallet of boxes. The fit anxiety from Consideration returns as assembly anxiety. (Lightly evidenced in call data, which rarely captures post-delivery; anchored on the fit logic: "always gonna fit if designed correctly.")

GAF action

Deliver the "designed correctly, so it fits" promise physically. Proactive assembly support turns a daunting unbox into the first workout — the real activation metric — and it sets up Phase 2.

Metric: Delivery-to-first-workout time; inbound support contacts per order.
05Grow (Phase 2)"I've outgrown the starter kit"

State of mind: confident, capable, wanting more. The starter setup did its job; now there is upgrade appetite (more plates, a bench, an attachment, flooring). Force USA plates, dumbbells and bumper plates are the top add-to-cart items site-wide — classic Phase 2 attachments.

The economics. Home Gym Builders reorder (frequency 1.65 sales-assisted, 1.99 online). Phase 2 attach baseline is 21.4% over 36 months, target 40%+. The saved design is the reactivation asset — it already knows what they have and what completes the set.

GAF action

Self-Serve: reorder prompts from the saved design ("complete your setup"). Sales-Assisted: consultant reactivation on a sensible cadence. This is where the money phrase's second beat — "to grow it" — is earned.

Metric: Phase 2 attach rate (21.4% → 40%).
06Advocacy"you have to see my garage"

State of mind: proud. A home gym is a visible, social artefact, and the owner shows it off. This closes the loop: the cohort's largest discovery channel is existing-customer and referral — today's advocates are driving tomorrow's Consideration.

Dominant JTBD: Social / Status — signal the achievement, help my mates do the same.

GAF action

Make the referral easy and asked-for: the consultant asks at the right moment; self-serve makes the saved design shareable. Every referred customer re-enters at Trigger with pre-loaded trust — which is why sales-assisted HGBs are worth $1,271 each and compound.

Metric: Referral-attributed new customers (the third beat of the money phrase, "to refer the next one").

07 · What this tells the build

Five concrete priorities for the AI Gym Designer & site

All five are evidenced in the stages above.

01

Own the Consideration→Design handoff

"I just don't know what I want" is the most common state of mind, and it is a design problem. Route overwhelmed browsers (especially on /collections/all-in-one-trainers) into the Designer.

02

Space and fit are the wedge — ask for the space first

Sloped garages, 24-inch rack-depth limits, ceiling height are named constraints. A designer that takes the room's dimensions and guarantees fit removes the #1 barrier.

03

Freight and stock must be truthful and early

Shipping-cost surprise and stock ambiguity are the top website-confusion topics and the top unresolved objections. Show real freight and real stock before checkout, not after.

04

Design for two people

The household decision is real. The saved design has to be shareable with a partner — 15%+ are joint decisions and the rest still get run past a spouse.

05

Save every design as the Phase 2 and referral asset

The saved design powers reorder prompts (Grow) and shareable proof (Advocacy). It is the object that makes the money phrase literally true.
