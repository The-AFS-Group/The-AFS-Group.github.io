June is 3.3× the quietest month. For this customer Black Friday is not a general traffic event, it is one of only two moments when project-mode buyers actually commit.

One number to handle carefully. You may know the Home Gym Builder as "$1,271 median profit, 2.5× online". That comes from the archetype model, which is a different population from the cart-rule cohort we now count. On the cohort we count, the assisted premium is 1.33×, not 2.5×, and the median gross profit is higher, not lower. Do not quote "$1,271 per home gym build" — the builds we count are worth more than that. Which figure becomes the official one is an open decision.
