Background / Objective

As part of the Gym and Fitness / Revel partnership with the Queensland Maroons, we have one EDM feature to promote our products and competition to the Maroons Members database. This brief outlines the content, messaging, and structure for your design team to work from.

The suggested angle is introducing Gym and Fitness (Official Gym Equipment Supplier) and Revel (Official Recovery Partner) as official partners, with key focus on the official QRL bumper plates with CTA to buy on the Gym and Fitness website, plus entry into our Money Can't Buy competition.

We'll provide imagery and product shots for use in the email. Camp photography of players using the plates is preferred where available.

Important: The Money Can't Buy giveaway is run by Gym and Fitness and Revel, not QRL. QRL is providing some of the prizes. Please frame accordingly in the email.
Subject Line Options
  1. 1. Meet the official partners behind the Maroons' training gear
  2. 2. Exclusive Queensland Maroons Bumper Plates - now available
  3. 3. The gear behind the Maroons' Origin campaign
Purpose

Introduce Gym and Fitness (Official Gym Equipment Supplier) and Revel (Official Recovery Partner) as official partners of the Queensland Maroons. Drive traffic to the QRL Bumper Plates product listing and the Gym and Fitness / Revel Money Can't Buy competition entry.

Suggested Copy (adapt to your template and tone)

Section 1 - Partner Introduction

Gym and Fitness and Revel are proud official partners of the Queensland Maroons for the 2026 Origin Campaign. As the Official Gym Equipment Supplier and Official Recovery Partner, they're helping the squad prepare for battle on and off the field.

Section 2 - Queensland Maroons Bumper Plates (Hero Product)

Train with the same plates the boys use at Camp Maroon. The official Queensland Maroons Bumper Plates are now available to pre-order exclusively through Gym and Fitness. Limited run, once they're gone, they're gone.

CTA: PRE-ORDER NOW [GAF product listing URL - TBC]

Section 3 - Revel Recovery (Official Recovery Partner)

As the Official Recovery Partner to the Queensland Maroons, Revel is helping the squad recover faster between sessions with infrared saunas and ice baths. The same recovery tools used by the Maroons are available for your home.

CTA: SHOP REVEL [Revel website URL - TBC]

Section 4 - Money Can't Buy Experience (GAF + Revel Giveaway)

Gym and Fitness and Revel are giving fans the chance to win the kind of Origin experience that no amount of money can get you. Gold seats at the final game. Sideline access at Camp Maroon. Signed memorabilia. This is as close to the action as it gets.

Grand Prize: 4x Gold Tickets to Game III in Brisbane (July 8th), signed match ball, signed jersey, and a set of QRL bumper plates.

Runner-Up Prizes: 2x Camp Maroon Sideline Experience Tickets each for two runners-up.

Free to enter. Competition closes June 23. Winners drawn June 24th.
