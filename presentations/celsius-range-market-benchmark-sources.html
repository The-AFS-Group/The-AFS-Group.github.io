Method. For each Celsius SKU the closest spec-comparable model was matched and its live advertised price captured on 8 Sep 2026, then the proposed RRP rated under / at / over the comparable band. Force USA and Gym & Fitness are AFS’s own brands, marked “(own)” and used as internal reference anchors, not independent competitors. Many comparators were captured mid-sale; the basis column notes the regular RRP where shown, and the gap to Celsius widens against regular pricing.
