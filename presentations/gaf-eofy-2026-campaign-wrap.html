The play

What we ran.

A focused EOFY sale built around the flagship all-in-one trainers, a curated deal landing page, and always-on tax-deadline urgency.

  • GWP
    Gift-with-purchase on the C20 + C10
    The proven conversion lever on our flagship all-in-ones — both finished the month in the top three products.
  • ★
    “Hot Deals” collection

What we learned

Takeaways & next.

1What worked

GWP on the flagships

The C20 + C10 gift-with-purchase again proved the strongest conversion lever — concentrating the offer on hero AIOs drove $456,359+ across the two SKUs.

2Watch

Margin held at 42%

Average discount stayed disciplined at 6.7%, protecting gross margin through a heavy-promotion month. GP landed 95% of budget without buying the number.

3Next

Bank the deadline surge

The 30 June spike shows tax-deadline urgency converts. Lock the “Hot Deals” + GWP playbook and creative winners as the repeatable EOFY template for FY27.
