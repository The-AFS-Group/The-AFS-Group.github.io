How to read this
  • Source: every call on the Revel Saunas line in Aircall. Calls with a real sales conversation (a transcript, enough back-and-forth, not voicemail) are analysed by AI; post-purchase service calls are mostly screened out before analysis.
  • Reasons, requests and not-closed reasons are grouped from the AI's free-text notes with keyword rules, so treat them as directional. The call log shows the original notes for every call.
  • Pricing offers and price pressure are detected from who said what in the transcript (Revel staff vs customer). Mentioning a running sale counts as an offer; a discount the rep puts on the table is shown separately.
  • Closed on the call means the AI marked the outcome as purchased during that conversation. Many sales complete later online, so this understates total conversion.
  • Competitors, competitor models and Revel's edge or drawback come from the AI's notes on each call. Beyond the 12 most-named brands, the rest are grouped as "Other brands"; hover that bar to see them.
  • Discovery questions are read from the rep's lines in the transcript, so a question phrased unusually can be missed. Use the coverage figures to compare people and topics rather than as exact counts.
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Times are Brisbane time. Data refreshes nightly.
