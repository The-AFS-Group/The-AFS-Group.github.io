How to read this
  • Calls: every Aircall call on the Revel line that the call analysis marked as after-sales (post-purchase) service, with a transcript. Voicemails and calls where the customer barely spoke are left out. Outbound calls include delivery bookings and calls to couriers, installers and technicians.
  • Issues, outcomes, approaches and mood are read from the transcript with keyword rules (in _pipelines/revel-service-insights/service.py), so treat them as directional and compare groups rather than reading single calls. The call log shows the evidence for each call.
  • Mood was checked against the AI's satisfaction rating on the few hundred service calls that have one: 50% of calls rated dissatisfied read as upset at some point vs 16 to 20% of satisfied ones, and of callers who opened upset, 42% of the very satisfied ended positive vs 10% of the dissatisfied. Polite customers who are quietly unhappy can still read as neutral.
  • Tickets: owner-assigned Customer Service tickets plus every Warranty and Forms ticket. The issue comes from the subject, claim form and first email; tickets with only a name as the subject stay "Other / unclear". HubSpot's own Category and Resolution fields are empty, so they can't be used.
  • First-contact resolution is HubSpot's flag for a ticket closed after one agent message. Time to first reply counts email replies only.
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Times are Brisbane time. Calls refresh nightly; tickets refresh nightly once a HubSpot token is set up (see the pipeline README), otherwise they show the date above.
